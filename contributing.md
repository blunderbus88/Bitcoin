
# Contributing

These principles are intended to reflect the community, not any one author. Disagreement, criticism, alternative wording, and personal perspectives are welcome.

## Open an Issue

Use an Issue to:

- Challenge or discuss an existing Principle
- Propose a new Principle
- Explain something you believe Bitcoin must preserve
- Share your perspective on why Bitcoin matters
- Raise philosophical, economic, or technical concerns

Please begin with an Issue so the idea can be discussed before changing the document.

## Submit a PR

Use a Pull Request when you have specific wording you would like incorporated into the document.

Please explain what you are changing and why

## The Goal

We are not looking for unanimous agreement on every sentence.

We are trying to identify a set of unifying Principles with community consensus, explain why they matter, and preserve that reasoning for the future.
